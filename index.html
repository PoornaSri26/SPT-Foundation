SPT corrections & analysis settings

N60 = Nf·CE·CB·CR·CS, then overburden CN and dilatancy corrections. When CR is enabled it is estimated from test depth (Bowles): <3 m → 0.75, 3–4 → 0.80, 4–6 → 0.85, 6–10 → 0.95, >10 → 1.00. The paper's app applies only CE, so defaults reproduce its results.
